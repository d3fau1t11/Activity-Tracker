Activity Tracker

Your private Telegram relay

Your PCs send reports and images directly to Telegram and use this relay when that connection fails. In your configured private chat, send /devices to choose a PC, then request reports, screenshots, and other tools. This site delivers commands to the selected PC.

Tracking status and clipboard collection are controlled through the app’s visible Windows tray icon. Clipboard collection requires a separate local opt-in.

Your images are stored privately with this relay, and queued deliveries retry automatically when Telegram becomes available.
